The field guide · Demo help

Make yourself at home.

A quick tour of the Stridewell storefront. Everything here is a safe, browser-based demonstration: explore freely, test the flows, and remember that no real money or messages are sent.

01 · DISCOVER

Browse footwear and apparel

Use the top navigation to switch between footwear, apparel, the studio page and this guide. Filter shoes by material and apparel by category, sort by price, and open a product for its details. Product photography is illustrative and credited where available.

02 · FIND IT

Search, wishlist and recently viewed

Use the magnifying-glass search to find items by name or details. Tap a heart to add or remove a favourite. Your dashboard collects saved wishlist items and recently viewed products. These lists are saved in this browser, not synced to an account server.

03 · YOUR BAG

Sizes, quantities and stock

Choose a size before adding a product. The bag shows selected items, sizes, quantities, subtotals and the total. Shoe stock is tracked per size: available counts update after a successful mock checkout, and sold-out sizes cannot be selected. Stock is simulated locally, so two different browsers do not share a live inventory.

04 · CHECKOUT

Test a preorder

You must be signed in as a customer to add products, preorder or check out. Enter demo contact and shipping details. Use card number 4242 4242 4242 4242 , any future expiry date and any 3-digit CVC to test the mock payment. No charge, shipment or real payment processing occurs. Promo code STRIDE10 applies the demo discount.

05 · ACCOUNT

Create, sign in and reset

The first sign-in attempt with a new email creates a browser-local account; later sign-ins need the same password. Passwords must be at least six characters. Remember me is checked by default and saves the email/password for autofill on this browser. Uncheck it to remove the saved autofill credentials; your account itself remains and you can still sign in by typing them. Forgot password? deletes only the account record for the email currently entered, allowing you to create it again with a new password. It does not wipe the whole site's storage or erase existing orders, cart, stock or settings. This is a demo reset, not verified identity recovery.

06 · DASHBOARD

Your customer area

After signing in, open Account to view your orders with live status tracking, wishlist and recently viewed products. Sign out from the dashboard to end the customer session. Your account record stays in this browser so you can return later.

07 · PERSONALISE

Store controls and newsletter

Switch the currency selector between USD, EUR, GBP and GHS, toggle the light/dark theme, and use the newsletter form to add a demo subscriber. After signup, a confirmation explains that your address was saved locally; no automated campaign is sent. Admin can view/export subscribers or prepare an email draft. The countdown and preorder progress are demo storefront elements.

08 · ADMIN

Explore the control room

Open Admin in the footer. Demo credentials: username admin , password stridewell2026 . The dashboard includes order management/status controls, customer/order views, CSV export, product visibility and pricing, insider posts, newsletter subscribers, countdown settings and an active-admin-browser count. The count only sees sessions sharing this exact browser storage/site origin.

Frequently asked questions
Does checkout charge a real card?

No. Checkout is a front-end simulation. Use the test card shown above. No payment provider is connected.

Why do I need an account to shop?

The demo requires a customer account before adding items, preordering or checkout. It keeps a simple local customer identity for the dashboard and order history.

Why can't an admin buy products?

Admin mode locks shopping so the person managing the demo cannot reserve the limited stock. Sign out of Admin to return to customer mode.

Why doesn't my order show on another device?

Each browser profile stores its own data. There is no shared backend or database connecting devices.

Does the newsletter send emails?

No automated campaign is sent. Subscribe stores your email in this browser's demo list. Admin can view/export that list and prepare an email draft in the email app.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the demo reset button on the account page. It deletes that one local account record so you can create it again. It is not secure identity verification.

Why don't the shoe angle buttons show the real heel or sole?

The product viewer uses one image and applies perspective transforms. It cannot create a true photograph of the heel, top or sole, so the controls are labelled as visual tilts.

Admin shopping restriction

This is intentional, not a bug: while the ADMIN badge is active, adding to bag, preordering and checkout are blocked. Sign out of Admin to remove the badge and restore normal customer shopping. Admins should not take limited stock from their own demo store.

What Remember me actually does

When checked, the demo stores a convenience copy of the email and password so the login form can autofill next time. When unchecked, that convenience copy is removed and the form will not autofill. The account record still exists, so unchecking it does not log you out, delete your account or reset your password. Because browser storage is readable by scripts and anyone with access to the browser profile, use only a made-up password.

Important demo limits

Accounts, passwords, login state, cart, wishlist, stock, orders, settings and newsletter entries are stored in localStorage in this browser only. They do not sync across devices or separate browsers. Passwords and admin credentials are visible in browser storage and page source, so never reuse real passwords or enter real customer/payment data. Inventory is a local simulation, not shared live stock. Payments, fulfilment, secure authentication and automated email delivery are not connected. Clearing this site's browser storage resets the local demo data.
